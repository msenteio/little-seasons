Where these ideas come from

Each activity is matched to the skills most babies are building at that age, using the CDC’s milestone checklists (the skills 75% or more of children show by each age) and the American Academy of Pediatrics. Many are adapted from Montessori infant and toddler practice: real objects, a calm uncluttered space, following the child’s lead, and letting her do it herself. The “serve and return” back-and-forth from Harvard’s Center on the Developing Child runs through all of them.

    Every baby develops on her own timeline, and age ranges here are guides, not rules. Always supervise, check that objects are too big to swallow, and ask your pediatrician about anything that worries you.
